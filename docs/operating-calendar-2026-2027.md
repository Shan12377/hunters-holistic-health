# PART 1: The Daily Operating System (every weekday, ~90 minutes total)

The needle-movers are boringly consistent. Protect these four blocks and the year takes care of itself.

### Block 1: Publish + Engage (8:00-8:45 AM ET) [YOU]
- Post the day's content at 8-9 AM (it's already written; Sunday batch, see below). Post, then stay: reply to every comment for 45 minutes.
- WHY: the first 60-90 minutes decide a post's reach (engagement velocity). A post you abandon is a post the algorithm abandons.
- EXACTLY HOW: content comes from the queue (scheduled via Metricool once set up); your job is only the replies. Links always in first comment. Never outsource this block; presence IS the product.

### Block 2: Community Founder Block (15 min, midday) [YOU]
- Reply to every new member intro. React to wins. Answer nothing clinical in public without the prescriber redirect.
- WHY: founder replies are the highest-retention act available at your size (community blueprint). This block is why members stay; the automations only bring them back to a room where you exist.

### Block 3: Pipeline 20 (20 min, your choice of slot) [YOU]
- Open automation-business/brain/PIPELINE.md. Do whatever the "closest path to cash" line says: 5 DMs, 1 proposal, 1 follow-up. Log it. Rewrite the line for tomorrow.
- WHY: the selling flywheel starves without daily reps. 20 minutes daily beats 3 hours on Friday.
- EXACTLY HOW: paste ORCHESTRATION-PROMPT.md into Claude, say "run it," execute what it drafts.

### Block 4: The 10-Minute Close (end of day) [YOU]
- Check Telegram (your briefing + alerts land there [AUTO-LIVE]). Approve anything in the Airtable queues (content, testimonials, knowledge harvests). Done.

**Daily total: ~90 minutes of human time. Everything else below is rhythm, not addition.**

# PART 6: Rationale Summary (why THIS calendar moves the needle)

1. Sequenced launches, one per month, each standing on the last: fixes → free tier → gates → proof → report → book → certification. Every launch inherits the previous one's assets. Parallel launches at your team size = three half-launches.
2. Daily blocks target the four levers directly: content+engagement = traffic; free tier + course = conversion [AUTO]; community rhythm + vault = churn; certification + price review = price. The Friday review names which lever gets next week's attention so you never work the wrong one.
3. Everything already written gets consumed on schedule: 7 days of posts, 8 emails, 12 modules, 12 session scripts, 84 content specs, pitch templates. The year's creative work is ~80% done; the calendar is mostly execution and review.
4. Your irreplaceable hours (clinical review, recording, replies, selling conversations) are the ONLY hours scheduled. Everything replaceable is automated or templated.
