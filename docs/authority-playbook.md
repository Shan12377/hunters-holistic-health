# The Authority Playbook
## The five moves you did not ask about, and the exact steps for each
### Prepared July 2026. Everything you have asked for so far (landing page, community, offers, marketing) makes you competitive. These five make you the default. They are ordered: each one feeds the next.

The pattern behind all five: competitors have content and offers too. The go-to person in any field owns four things nobody else has: a named idea, original data, other people's stages, and other people's credentials. You are positioned for all four and using none of them yet.

---

## Move 1: Own a Term. Coin "The GLP-1 Exit Strategy."

Nobody owns this phrase. You are already saying it (your LinkedIn bio, your Day 4 post). Millions are on these medications, coverage is discussed everywhere, and the single most-searched anxiety is what happens when I stop. Whoever defines that conversation owns it. Category kings do not compete for attention; they get cited.

**Exact steps:**

1. Week 1: Write the definitive page: "The GLP-1 Exit Strategy: The Complete Guide" at huntersholistichealth.com/glp-1-exit-strategy. 3,000+ words. Define the term in the first paragraph in one quotable sentence: "A GLP-1 exit strategy is the education and habit plan built BEFORE the last dose, so the patterns that raised your weight are not waiting at the finish line." Include the Wilding 2021 NEJM citation, the lean mass data, an FAQ section, and FAQ schema markup. Run it through your blog publisher pipeline.
2. Week 1: File the paperwork habit: use "GLP-1 Exit Strategy" with consistent capitalization in every post, bio, and talk from now on. Same consistency rule as ROOTS™. (Ask your trademark attorney whether it is protectable or better left as an open category term you simply lead. Category terms often spread FASTER unprotected.)
3. Week 2: Record one 90-second video defining the term. Pin it on every profile.
4. Weeks 2 to 8: Every GLP-1 post you publish uses the phrase and links the guide in the first comment. You are training humans and search engines simultaneously.
5. Month 3: Expand the guide into a short book (30,000 words, self-published on Amazon KDP, $200 to $500 for cover and formatting). Title: "The GLP-1 Exit Strategy." A book is the authority artifact podcasts and journalists check for. It does not need to sell; it needs to exist and be good.
6. Compliance rail: the term is educational by construction ("education and habit plan"). Keep every definition free of treatment language. Never frame it as medication guidance.

**You have won when:** people you never met use the phrase and someone else's post says "as Dr. Hunter calls it."

## Move 2: Publish Original Data. The Annual Report Nobody Else Can Write.

Every guru has opinions. Almost nobody your size has DATA. You own a platform that generates anonymized, aggregate behavioral data: consistency scores, streak survival, which habits move which self-reported numbers, what people misunderstand most. That is publishable original research, and original research is the #1 magnet for backlinks, press quotes, and AI citations.

**Exact steps:**

1. Now: Add one question to your free Metabolic Pattern Check flow: "Did anyone ever explain what this number means?" Yes/No. That single field becomes your headline stat: "X% of people with borderline numbers say nobody explained them."
2. Month 1: Verify your Terms of Service permit aggregate anonymized research use of platform data (yours already gestures at privacy-first; have the attorney confirm the clause). Non-PHI layer only, aggregates only, minimum group size 20, no individual stories without written permission.
3. Months 1 to 3: Collect. You need only a few hundred data points for a v1 report.
4. Month 4: Publish "The Metabolic Education Gap Report 2026" as a free PDF and a web page: 5 to 7 findings, 3 charts, one quotable stat per finding. Design it clean; it will be screenshot.
5. Month 4, launch week: One LinkedIn carousel per finding (5 posts). Pitch the report to 20 health journalists and 20 newsletters with a 3-sentence email: the stat, why now, link. Journalists do not want your opinion; they want your number.
6. Every year: repeat. "The 2027 Report" becomes an anticipated event, and you become the citation.

**You have won when:** an article you did not write cites your stat with your name.

## Move 3: Borrow 100 Stages. The Podcast and Press Circuit.

You are building your own audience one post at a time. The go-to shortcut is other people's audiences. Fifty podcast interviews in 12 months puts you in front of hundreds of thousands of pre-warmed listeners and builds a Google/AI footprint of your name attached to your topic. Zero followers required to start.

**Exact steps:**

1. Week 1: Build the one-page media kit (single web page): photo, 3 bios (50, 100, 250 words), your 5 talkable topics ("Why GLP-1 users need an exit strategy," "What 90 seconds at the pharmacy counter taught me about healthcare," "The pharmacist teaching healthcare workers to build AI tools"), 3 sample questions per topic, logos/credentials, contact. Add the umbrella slogan.
2. Week 2: List 100 target shows in a spreadsheet: 40 health/wellness podcasts, 30 pharmacy/healthcare professional shows, 20 women-in-business and Caribbean community shows, 10 AI/tech shows (Lane 2). Rank by audience relevance, not size. A 500-listener show of pharmacists beats a 50,000-listener general show.
3. Week 2 onward: Pitch 10 per week, personal 4-sentence email: one line proving you listened, the specific angle for THEIR audience, one credibility line (PharmD + reversed my own condition + built the platform), the media kit link. Track replies in the same sheet. Expect 10 to 20% booking rates with real personalization.
4. Sign up for expert-source services (Qwoted, Featured, SourceBottle) and answer 3 journalist queries a week in your lanes. Fifteen minutes each. These become "as seen in" logos.
5. Every interview: say the term (Move 1), cite your data (Move 2), and give ONE call to action: the free Metabolic Clarity Kit. Never list all five businesses; that resets you to zero in the listener's memory.
6. Repurpose every appearance: 3 clips for social, one quote graphic, an "Interviews" page on your site.
7. Compliance rail: in interviews you are always the educator. Rehearse the pivot sentence for medical-advice questions: "That is a conversation for their prescriber. What I teach is what nobody has time to explain after the prescription is written."

**You have won when:** shows start pitching YOU.

## Move 4: Become the Answer AI Gives. The GEO Moat.

In 2026 the front door of your field is not just Google; it is ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews answering "what happens when I stop semaglutide" and "what does borderline blood pressure mean." Those engines cite pages that are structured, definitional, consistent, and attached to a clear expert entity. Your competitors write vibes. You can write citations.

**Exact steps:**

1. Week 1: Entity cleanup. Same name, credentials, and bio phrasing everywhere (site, LinkedIn, podcast pages, book author page). Add Person schema and Organization schema to huntersholistichealth.com: name, credentials, sameAs links to every profile. AI engines resolve you into one entity; make it easy.
2. Weeks 2 to 6: Build the Glossary: 25 short definitional pages, one term each ("What is insulin resistance," "What is waist-to-height ratio," "What is a GLP-1 exit strategy," "What is HOMA-IR"). Format each identically: one-sentence definition first (the quotable answer), 300 to 600 words of plain-language mechanism, an FAQ block with FAQ schema, the educational disclaimer, a link to the relevant free tool. Definition-first pages are what AI engines lift.
3. Ongoing: Every blog post gets: a question as the H1 or first H2, the direct answer in the first 2 sentences, FAQ schema, and a named-author byline with credentials. Your blog publisher skill already targets this; enforce it.
4. Month 2: Citations flywheel: the Move 2 report and Move 3 interviews create third-party pages naming you next to your topic. That co-occurrence is what makes engines treat you as the authority. The moves compound; this is why they are ordered.
5. Monthly, 15 minutes: ask ChatGPT and Perplexity your 10 money questions ("best resource for GLP-1 exit planning," "who teaches metabolic health education online"). Log whether you appear. This is your GEO scoreboard.

**You have won when:** an AI answer recommends your framework or tools unprompted.

## Move 5: Certify Others. The ROOTS™ Educator Certification.

You already said the endgame: "mine will be the blueprint... implement for others later." The field-defining move is not serving more clients; it is credentialing other professionals in YOUR method. IFM did it for functional medicine. Every certified educator pays you, evangelizes you, and makes ROOTS the standard they practice under. This is how a framework becomes a field.

**Exact steps:**

1. Now to Month 6: Prerequisites first. Do not launch this until: the platform community retains well (community-blueprint metrics green), you have 10+ documented member outcomes, and Moves 1 to 3 are running. A certification sells on proof.
2. Month 3: Validate demand with zero build: one LinkedIn post to Lane 2: "Considering opening a certification for health professionals who want to teach the ROOTS™ Framework with their own clients. Serious interest only: comment CERTIFY." 30+ qualified comments = build. Fewer = wait and keep building proof.
3. Month 4: Legal structure with your attorney: trademark license terms, what certificants may and may not claim (they are educators using a licensed framework, never "treating"), scope-of-practice language per THEIR license type, annual renewal, revocation terms. This document is the product's spine; do not skip it.
4. Months 5 to 6: Build the v1 program from what exists: your curriculum + teach-the-teacher modules (how to run the education, the compliance rails, the community playbook from your blueprint doc) + case study requirement + exam. 8 weeks, cohort-based, live sessions. Price: $3,000 to $5,000, application only, 10 to 15 seats for cohort one. Founding cohort gets lifetime pricing lock; their results become the sales page.
5. Month 6+: Certified educators get: directory listing on your site (which also feeds Move 4: dozens of professional pages naming ROOTS™), a members-only educator community (your own blueprint, re-applied), and optional licensing of your platform tech later. That last piece is where Pharmacy Decoder-style SaaS margins eventually live.
6. Rule: never certify faster than you can maintain quality. One bad certificant using treatment language under your mark is a brand and legal event. The attorney-drafted license plus an annual audit protects you.

**You have won when:** job listings and bios say "ROOTS™ Certified" without your involvement.

Time cost honestly stated: Moves 1 and 4 are front-loaded writing (the blog pipeline does the heavy lifting). Move 3 is 2 hours a week forever. Move 2 is a quarterly push. Move 5 is a real product build; it earns its place by being the one that makes you the standard instead of a standout.

One warning as your thinking partner: five moves plus a platform plus daily content is more than one person executes well simultaneously. The sequence above is designed so months 1 to 3 need only writing and pitching. If anything slips, protect Moves 1 and 3 first; a named idea repeated on a hundred stages is the fastest path to "undeniable" ever discovered.
