## Part 1: Brand Architecture (fix this first, it decides everything else)

You have five properties. The mistake would be marketing five brands. You market ONE: Dr. Shallanda Hunter. The businesses are proof of what you do, not separate voices.

**The umbrella:** Dr. Shallanda Hunter, PharmD. The pharmacist who teaches what the 10-minute appointment can't.

**Two audiences, two lanes, one person:**

| Lane | Audience | Properties | Primary channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lane 1: Metabolic health education | Everyday people, GLP-1 users, "borderline numbers" people, Caribbean community | Hunter's Holistic Health, ROOTS™, GLPRoot | Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, email. LinkedIn secondary. |
| Lane 2: Healthcare pros building with AI | Pharmacists, nurses, clinicians, especially women | I Can Teach You AI, DeIDGuard, Pharmacy Decoder | LinkedIn primary. YouTube later. |

**The 1-1-1 discipline (from your own Offer OS rules):** under $1M per business, one avatar, one product, one channel. Right now the platform launch is the priority, so the default split is 70% Lane 1, 30% Lane 2. When you promote a Pharmacy Decoder launch or an AI cohort, flip the split for that campaign week, then flip back. You never change voices, only topics.

**Content never sells five things at once.** One post, one lane, one CTA. Your bio is where all properties live together.

**The repurpose chain (one idea, five outputs, this is how you post daily without creating daily):**
Blog post or core idea → LinkedIn text post → Instagram carousel → Pinterest pin → email section → (later) YouTube script. Write once on Sunday, slice all week.

**Non-negotiable mechanics:** links in the FIRST COMMENT on LinkedIn, never the body (60% reach penalty). No hashtag spam anywhere, three max on Instagram, zero on LinkedIn. Post 8 to 9 AM Eastern. Reply to every comment within 60 minutes of posting; that window decides reach.

**Content mix, every week (4-1-1 discipline):** 40% education, 30% personal story, 20% opinion/thought leadership, 10% promotion. One promotional post per week, maximum. Every non-promo post still carries the free Kit link in the first comment. Value 10 times, ask once.

## Part 6: The Daily Operating System (45 to 60 minutes)

Morning (30 min): post at 8 to 9 AM Eastern. Reply to every comment for the first hour. 10 thoughtful comments (15+ words) on target-audience posts.
Midday (10 min): 10 more comments or connection requests. Check profile viewers, message the warm ones: "Saw you stopped by. Looking for something specific or browsing?"
Evening (15 min): DMs to warm leads only (commenters, Kit opt-ins, past interest). Three-step flow: get a reply, give value, then ask. Never cold pitch.

Weekly (Sunday, 90 min): write the week's core idea, slice into the 7 formats, schedule. Review last week: double down on whatever got saves and comments, drop what got neither.

## Part 7: Compliance Rails for All Content (non-negotiable)

- Educator language only: clients not patients, sessions not consultations, educate/support/optimize, never treat/cure/manage/diagnose.
- Every patient-facing educational post carries: "Educational only, not medical advice." Short form is fine on social.
- The regain statistic always cites Wilding et al., 2021, NEJM. No unsourced statistics, ever.
- GLPulse, myglp1cost.com, glp1checker.com mentions require the founder #Disclosure line. Affiliate links get #Affiliate adjacent to the link.
- Supplement content carries the DSHEA disclaimer.
- Never advise starting or stopping any medication.
- No em dashes. No AI-tell vocabulary. Read every post aloud before publishing: if it sounds like a brand, rewrite until it sounds like you.

## Part 8: Measure These Five (weekly, nothing else)

1. Kit opt-ins (the number that matters most; everything feeds it)
2. LinkedIn: saves + comments per post (ignore likes and impressions)
3. Instagram: saves and shares per post
4. Email list growth and open rate
5. DM conversations started with warm leads

Thirty days in, the posts with the most saves become your carousel and email topics. The market tells you the content calendar after week four. Listen to it.
