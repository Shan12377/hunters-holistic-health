## Part 0: The Two Kinds of Gates (never confuse them)

| Gate type | Controlled by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Tier gates (money) | What they PAY | Foundation vs Program vs VIP features: unlimited Meal Guard, live sessions, 1-on-1s. Already built. |
| Level gates (engagement) | What they DO | Points earned through participation unlock bonus depth. This doc. |

Level gates sit INSIDE membership. A Foundation member and a VIP member both climb the same levels. That is what makes levels feel fair and makes participation, not payment, the status game.

## Part 1: What to NEVER Lock (the safety floor)

These stay open to every member at every level, permanently:

1. All five free tools and every tracking tool (Daily Command Center, BP, blood sugar, supplement log). Gating tracking kills the habit loop that everything else depends on.
2. The core lessons of any assigned protocol. If following their protocol safely requires it, it is never behind a gate.
3. All safety content, disclaimers, and "talk to your prescriber" education.
4. Progress Reports (the printable summaries they bring to their own providers). Gating those gates their healthcare. Never.
5. Community reading and posting. Participation is how points are EARNED; locking it starves the system.

## Part 4: The Comeback Structure (how each unlock creates return visits)

Every vault item follows the same loop, and this is what makes gating retention instead of decoration:

1. **The unlock moment:** in-app celebration + email ("You just unlocked X, here's the 90-second version of why it matters").
2. **Consume:** the item itself (built on the template in Part 6).
3. **Apply:** every item ends with ONE action that requires the app: log it, track it for 7 days, post the result in the community.
4. **Report back:** the action generates a post or a tracked number, which earns points, which climbs the next level. The loop closes.
5. **The teaser:** every vault item ends by naming the next locked item and the level it lives at. Curiosity has a progress bar.

### The Meal Sequencing Masterclass
**Advanced Module | Unlocks at Level 4 | 8-minute read + 7-day experiment**

**Eat the same plate. Change the order. Cut the spike.**

You were told to give up rice. Nobody told you the order you eat your plate in changes what that rice does to your blood sugar.

I grew up on plates where everything had its place: the provision, the protein, the vegetables. Turns out the ORDER those hit your stomach is one of the most studied tricks in metabolic research, and almost nobody teaches it.

**The mechanism, plainly:**
When vegetables and protein arrive in your stomach first, they slow gastric emptying, the rate your stomach releases food into the small intestine. Carbohydrates that arrive behind that buffer get absorbed slower. Slower absorption means a lower, flatter glucose curve from the exact same food. Research on meal sequencing has shown post-meal glucose reductions in the range of 30 to 40% when carbohydrates come last instead of first, with effects in people with normal glucose, prediabetes, and type 2 diabetes. Fiber first also triggers earlier release of your own GLP-1, the same hormone family the famous medications work through. Your body makes it free; sequence helps you use it.

**The method (one rule, three steps):**
1. Vegetables first. A real portion, not a garnish. Callaloo, salad, steamed greens, whatever your kitchen does.
2. Protein and fats second. The fish, the chicken, the beans.
3. Starches and sugars last. The rice, the provision, the bread, the sweet thing.
Same plate. Same food. New order. That's the whole method.

**What this is NOT:** a replacement for anything your provider has you doing, a guarantee of any specific number, or a reason to skip medications. It is one evidence-informed lever, and it costs nothing.

**Your 7-day experiment (this is the part that counts):**
For the next 7 days, sequence ONE meal per day, whichever meal is usually your heaviest. If you have a glucose meter and already test, note your 2-hour post-meal readings on sequenced vs. normal days in the Blood Sugar tracker. No meter? Track your 1-2 hour energy crash in the Daily Command Center energy score instead.
Day 7: post your experience in the Wins room with the tag #SequenceExperiment. What changed, what didn't, what surprised you. Your data point teaches the whole community.

**Next on your path:** at Level 5 the Replay Vault opens, including the live session where members brought their sequencing results and we walked through what their curves showed.

*Educational content only, not medical advice. Individual responses vary. Discuss glucose patterns and any concerns with your healthcare provider.*

## Part 6: The Template (build every vault item from this)

```
TITLE: [Benefit in their words, not a topic label]
SLOT: [Module name] | Unlocks at Level [N] | [time to consume] + [experiment length]

HOOK (1-2 lines): the thing they believed that isn't quite true, or the
result hiding in plain sight.

PERSONAL BRIDGE (1-3 lines): her story, a client pattern, or a cultural
anchor. Real, specific, unlabeled.

THE MECHANISM (2-4 short paragraphs): teach ONE mechanism in plain language.
Name the process. Cite the research range honestly, no cherry-picked
best case. If a statistic appears, its citation appears.

THE METHOD (numbered, 3 steps max): so simple it fits on a sticky note.

WHAT THIS IS NOT (2-3 lines): the compliance paragraph written as care,
not legalese. Sets the boundary with the prescriber redirect where relevant.

THE EXPERIMENT (the comeback engine): one action, one tracking target IN
THE APP, one duration, one community share with a tag. This is mandatory.
A vault item without an experiment is content; with one, it's retention.

NEXT ON YOUR PATH (1 line): name the next locked item and its level.

DISCLAIMER: the standard educational disclaimer, always.
```

Rules: 6 to 10 minute read maximum. One mechanism per item, never three. Every item passes clinical review (her), compliance review (legal-compliance skill), and voice check (read aloud) before it ships. Supplement-related items additionally carry the DSHEA disclaimer and never include dosing without her explicit sign-off.

## Part 7: The Free Floor (where the free plan fits, and why it is the growth engine)

The research is consistent: free community as top-of-funnel is the fastest 2026 growth path, and gamified communities retain roughly 24% more members per quarter. Combine those and the design writes itself: free members join the SAME level ladder as paid members, starting at the ground floor. One ladder, one points system, no separate "free gamification."

### How it works

1. **Free members (the Lobby, from the Metabolic Clarity Kit) earn real points** from their limited surfaces: the intro post, likes received on it, daily tool usage streaks, and course-email actions. Capped earning, real climbing. A free member can genuinely reach Level 2 in their first week of showing up.

2. **Level 1 free unlock (day one):** the Pattern-to-Lesson preview: their tool results mapped to which ROOTS lesson answers them, plus ROOTS Lesson One. Instant taste of the teaching.

3. **Level 2 for a free member is the conversion moment, by design.** They EARN Level 2 through engagement, and the unlock screen says: "You reached Level 2. Members at this level open the Quick Wins Vault (47 recipes + the meal collections). Your level and points carry over the moment you join." They hit the gate with the key already in their hand and only the membership door between them. This converts engagement into intent at the exact peak of both. A free member who earned Level 2 is the highest-intent lead the business can generate.

4. **Points NEVER reset on upgrade.** Carrying progress across the paywall is the entire psychological trick: they are not buying access, they are claiming what they already earned. Any design that resets progress at upgrade burns the mechanism.

5. **Free members see the full level map**, every locked item's title and level, exactly like paid members. Visible locked doors, floor to penthouse. The map IS the sales page.

6. **What free members give the system back:** density (the Lobby full of intros and wins makes the community look alive to every visitor), content (their questions feed the FAQ, the vault topics, and the transcript-to-knowledge pipeline), and referrals (a "bring a friend to the Lobby" action can itself earn points, making growth a point-earning behavior).

### The one guardrail

Free earning surfaces stay capped (intro, likes, tool streaks) so a free member cannot farm to Level 4 without ever joining. The ladder is generous on floor one and honest above it: from Level 2 upward, unlocks require membership plus the level. Earned but locked converts; unreachable frustrates; free-everything starves the paid tier. The cap is what keeps it in the first category.

### The free-floor loop in one line
Kit opt-in → Lobby intro (points) → tool streak (points) → Level 2 earned → vault door with their key in hand → Foundation, progress intact → the paid ladder from Part 3 takes over.
