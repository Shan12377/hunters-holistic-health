# The Offer Architecture
## Hunter's Holistic Health: Free through $4,997, one ladder, one logic
### Prepared July 2026. Built on the Irresistible Offer OS principles (Value Equation, Grand Slam stack, bonuses over discounts, real scarcity, risk reversal) applied to your live tiers.

---

## Part 1: The Spine (read this first, everything hangs on it)

Every tier answers the same four Value Equation questions, just at higher intensity:

- Dream outcome: "I finally understand what my body is telling me, and I know what to do about it."
- Certainty: PharmD + CFNMP credentials, her own reversal story, citations, and (as they accumulate) member proof.
- Time delay: how fast they get their first "aha."
- Effort: how little they have to figure out alone.

The ladder logic: **each tier delivers a complete win AND reveals the problem only the next tier solves.** That is what makes the flow feel natural instead of salesy.

| Tier | The win it delivers | The problem it reveals | Which the next tier solves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | "Now I see my pattern" | "But I don't know WHY or what to do" | Foundation teaches the why |
| Foundation $37 | "Now I understand and can track it" | "But I keep falling off alone" | Program adds accountability + live access |
| Program $97 | "Now I'm consistent and supported" | "But my situation has specifics a group can't cover" | VIP adds 1-on-1 attention |
| VIP $997 | "Now I have her eyes on MY patterns" | "I want full immersion, everything mapped" | Overhaul is the complete engagement |
| Overhaul $4,997 | The everything tier | (top of ladder) | Renewal / alumni community |

One rule enforced everywhere: never sell the next tier by making the current one feel insufficient. Sell it as the New Opportunity the current win unlocked. "You've mastered X, which means you're ready for Y."

## Part 2: The Free Tier Grand Slam Offer

You currently have free tools with no account and no free tier. That is the gap. The free tier is where the Grand Slam principle matters MOST, because free is where trust is built and emails are owned.

### The name (Magic Naming)

**"The Metabolic Clarity Kit"** with the free membership called **"The Lobby."**
Alternative names if you want to test: "The ROOTS™ Starter," "The 5-Minute Clarity Kit."

### The stack (everything already exists or is one n8n workflow away)

Presented on the page as a stack, each line named and framed:

1. **The Five Clarity Tools** (BP Zone, Blood Sugar Zone, Metabolic Pattern Check, Waist-to-Height, Hormone Snapshot). Already built. Framed: "the five checks a functional medicine educator runs first."
2. **Your Pattern Snapshot, saved.** A free account saves their tool results so they can see them in one place. This is the reason to create an account instead of staying anonymous. Limit: current snapshot plus 7 days of history. (Small build: reuse existing tracking tables with a free-tier flag.)
3. **The "Understand Your Numbers" 7-Day Email Course.** One number explained per day in plain language: fasting glucose, BP, waist-to-height, energy crashes, cravings, sleep, and day 7 ties them together with ROOTS. (Build: one n8n sequence. This is the workhorse asset.)
4. **ROOTS Lesson One, unlocked.** The first curriculum lesson free. Lets them taste the teaching quality. (Build: free-tier gate on one lesson.)
5. **The Lobby room.** Read access to the community Wins room plus the ability to post one introduction. They see real people winning; they cannot fully participate. Visible locked doors are the ascension engine. (Aligns with community-blueprint Part 9.)

### The offer copy (page-ready, compliant)

> **Get The Metabolic Clarity Kit. Free.**
> Five educational tools, your saved pattern snapshot, a 7-day plain-English email course on what your numbers mean, the first ROOTS lesson, and a seat in the Lobby to watch members do the work.
> No credit card. No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.
> *Subscribe for weekly functional health education. By subscribing, you agree to receive educational content. Not medical advice.*

### Why this is Grand Slam

- Dream outcome: clarity, delivered in minutes (tools) and days (email course).
- Certainty: they experience her teaching before paying a dollar.
- Time delay: first insight in under 5 minutes.
- Effort: email address. That's it.
- The reveal: the Kit shows the pattern. It deliberately does not explain root causes, does not give the full curriculum, does not give tracking history, does not give community participation. That is the Foundation-shaped hole, felt, not stated.

## Part 3: The Paid Tiers, Rebuilt as Stacked Offers

Current pricing cards list features. Features inform; stacks convert. Same tiers, same prices, restructured presentation.

### Foundation, $37/mo: "The Self-Guided System"

Core: full ROOTS curriculum, VitaPlate AI Meal System, Metabolic Tracking Suite, Weekly Report Card, full community participation.

Bonuses (each kills one named objection):
- **Bonus 1: The Supplement Safety Starter.** Educational guide to how common supplements interact with common medications, PharmD educator-designed. Kills "I don't know if my stack is safe." *Requires the FDA DSHEA disclaimer on the asset.*
- **Bonus 2: The 30-Day Consistency Challenge.** Structured first-month challenge with points and recognition. Kills "I never stick with things."
- **Bonus 3: The Pattern-to-Lesson Map.** Their free-tier Pattern Snapshot maps to which ROOTS lessons to start with. Kills "where do I even start." (Build: simple mapping logic, high perceived personalization.)

Risk reversal: "Cancel in two clicks from Settings, anytime. No emails, no phone calls, no guilt."

### Program, $97/mo: "The Accountability Tier" (Most Popular stays)

Core: everything in Foundation, unlimited Meal Guard, live monthly group sessions with Dr. Hunter, Weekly Pulse AI, private cohort room, priority content access.

Bonuses:
- **Bonus 1: The Session Vault.** Every past live session recording, searchable. Kills "what if I miss the live."
- **Bonus 2: First-Week Momentum Call.** One group onboarding session for new Program members each month. Kills "I'll get lost."
- **Bonus 3: Ask-It Box.** Submit one question per month guaranteed to be answered in the live session. Kills "my question won't get picked."

Risk reversal: same cancel-anytime language, loud, directly under the button.

### VIP, $997/mo: "The Intensive"

Core: everything in Program, 2x monthly private 1-on-1 educational sessions, personalized ROOTS educational wellness roadmap, direct educator messaging with 24-hour response.

Offer rules for this tier:
- Application, not checkout. High-ticket converts through conversation (CLOSER framework), not buttons. The current "Apply for VIP" is right; the application should ask about their goals, history, and commitment (Foot-in-the-Door + qualification).
- Scarcity: "10 active seats" stays ONLY if literally enforced with a real counter. Show "7 of 10 seats filled" if true. If not enforced, remove it today; fake scarcity at $997 is a reputation event.
- Bonus: **The Roadmap Session.** The first session builds their complete educational roadmap so they leave session one with the plan in hand. Time-to-value: one week.

### VIP Overhaul, $4,997: "The 6-Month Functional Overhaul"

Application-only, 3 active clients, positioned as the Rejection-Then-Retreat anchor: it appears first in the pricing display (as it does now), making every other tier feel accessible. Rules:
- Never discount it. Price integrity at the top protects the whole ladder.
- The 3-client limit must be real and stated with a waitlist when full. A waitlist at the top tier is the strongest social proof on the whole page.
- Add: **Alumni status.** Overhaul graduates keep Program access for 6 months after. Kills "what happens when it ends" and feeds long-term community.

## Part 4: The Ascension Flow (where each offer appears, so the ladder actually moves)

An offer nobody sees at the right moment converts nobody. Placement map:

| Trigger moment | What they see | Ascends to |
|---|---|---|
| Free tool result shown (anonymous) | "Save this number + get the 7-day course" inline capture | Free |
| Free account, day 3 of email course | Email: Lesson One unlocked, "here's what the full curriculum covers" | Foundation |
| Free account, day 7 (course complete) | Email: the Pattern-to-Lesson Map preview + Foundation offer with full stack | Foundation |
| Free member tries to post/comment beyond intro in Lobby | "Full community is a membership feature" modal with Foundation stack | Foundation |
| Foundation member hits 5-query Meal Guard cap | "You're using this like a Program member" upgrade prompt | Program |
| Foundation member completes ROOTS Phase 1 | Congratulations screen + Program invitation framed as earned readiness | Program |
| Program member attends 2nd live session | Personal-tone email: "You show up. That's who VIP is built for. Want to talk?" | VIP |
| Program/VIP member with complex goals on a 1-on-1 | Conversation only, CLOSER framework, never a button | Overhaul |
| Any member cancels | Downgrade path offered before cancel completes (Program → Foundation → Free), never lose them to zero | Retention |

Two enforcement rules:
1. One ascension prompt per moment, never stacked. A member should never see two upgrade asks on one screen.
2. Every prompt leads with what they DID (commitment and consistency), not what they lack.

### The pass checks (run before any offer ships)

1. Value check: would a stranger say the stack is obviously worth 10x the price? If not, add value, never cut price.
2. Truth check: every scarcity claim, seat count, and bonus exists and is enforced. FTC and trust both.
3. Compliance check: educator language throughout, no outcome guarantees, no "treat/cure/manage," DSHEA disclaimer on any supplement asset, testimonial disclaimer on proof. (Full rules: legal-compliance skill.)
4. Cannibalization check: does the free tier give away the paid tier's core? Free shows patterns; paid explains and supports. If a free asset starts converting worse-not-better, it crossed the line.
5. One-problem check: each bonus kills exactly one objection you can name. A bonus that kills nothing is clutter.
6. Reveal check: can you state, in one sentence, the problem this tier surfaces that the next tier solves? If not, the ladder is broken at that rung.

## Part 6: Value Anchoring Without Fake Numbers

The guru move is "$2,000 value, yours for $37." FTC requires value claims be defensible. Your compliant versions:

- Comparison anchoring: "A single 1-on-1 educational session runs $250. The Program gives you live access every month for $97." True and checkable.
- Cost-of-alternative anchoring: "One functional medicine intake elsewhere: $400 to $600, before follow-ups." Cite a real range.
- Time anchoring: "Built from 6 years of PharmD practice and her own reversal journey."
- Never print a made-up "total value: $4,300" unless each line item is genuinely sold separately at that price somewhere.
