# 04: FAQ (Real Questions, Educator-Scope Answers)
Last verified: July 2026. Every answer is educational; none is medical advice. These are the canonical short answers; long versions live on the site as glossary/FAQ pages with FAQ schema.

## About the business

**Is this medical care?**
No, and that is the point. Hunter's Holistic Health is an educational platform. Dr. Hunter is a PharmD acting as a functional medicine educator, not your prescribing physician or clinical pharmacist. The education is designed to make your conversations with your own providers better, not to replace them.

**What do I get that my doctor doesn't give me?**
Time and explanation. Doctors get about 10 minutes per visit; that is not enough to teach anyone how their metabolic patterns work. The platform teaches the why behind your numbers and gives you tools to track what moves them.

**Who is this for?**
People told their numbers are "borderline" and sent home with no next steps; people managing blood pressure or blood sugar who want to understand what drives them; people on GLP-1 medications who want an exit strategy; anyone who wants functional and nutritional medicine education with the science cited.

**How much does it cost?**
The Metabolic Clarity Kit is free. Platform memberships run $37 to $997 per month. Private 1-on-1 educational engagements start at a $349 Supplement Stack Audit. Full transparent pricing: see 02-services-and-pricing.md (public pricing page mirrors it).

**Can I cancel?**
Monthly memberships: anytime, two clicks, from Settings. Annual: refundable within 14 days, pausable up to 3 months after.

**Do I need lab work to start?**
No. The free tools use numbers you already have (a BP cuff reading, a glucose meter reading, a tape measure). Private engagements can educationally contextualize labs you already have from your own providers.

## About the method

**What is the ROOTS Framework?**
See canonical definition in 03-frameworks.md. Five phases: Review, Optimize Nutrition, Optimize Biochemistry, Transform Lifestyle, Sustain and Adapt.

**What is a GLP-1 exit strategy?**
The education and habit plan built before the last dose, so the patterns that raised your weight are not waiting at the finish line. On average, people regain two-thirds of lost weight within a year of stopping (Wilding et al., 2021, NEJM). The strategy is education around the medication journey, never medication guidance.

**Do I have to give up my cultural foods?**
No. Meal order, portions, and pairing matter more than banning foods. The curriculum is built to work with real kitchens, including Caribbean ones, not against them.

**Will you tell me to stop my medication?**
Never. No content, session, or tool on this platform advises starting, stopping, or changing any medication. Those decisions belong with you and your prescriber. What you get here is the education to have that conversation well.

**Are supplements required?**
No. Supplement education (what things do, how they interact with medications) is part of the curriculum. These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA; no supplement discussed is intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

## About the tools and data

**What happens to my data?**
Privacy is the architecture: age only (never date of birth), no ads, no trackers, no data sales, AI never sees identified health records, one-click account deletion.

**Are the free tools a diagnosis?**
No. They give educational context using published reference ranges (AHA/ACC for blood pressure, ADA for glucose). Any reading of concern goes to your healthcare provider.
