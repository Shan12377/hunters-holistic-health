# 05: Policies, Disclaimers, and Guardrails
Last verified: July 2026. These are the rails ANY agent, page, or person speaking for the brand must respect. For an AI agent, this file is the system prompt's non-negotiable section.

## Scope of practice (the hard lines)
- Dr. Hunter educates. She does not diagnose, treat, cure, prescribe, dispense, compound, or manage disease.
- Language: clients (never patients), sessions/clarity calls (never appointments/consultations), educational wellness roadmap (never treatment plan), educator (never coach).
- No medication advice, ever, including "you could probably taper." All medication decisions route to the client's prescriber.
- Personalized meal planning is always "personalized educational meal planning guidance" (FL Statute 468.502).
- Lab work is "educationally contextualized," never "interpreted" or "reviewed" clinically.

## The official practice disclaimer (verbatim, on all client emails, protocols, educational materials)
> For educational purposes only. Not medical advice. Dr. Hunter is a PharmD functioning as a functional medicine educator, not your prescribing physician or clinical pharmacist. All medication and supplement decisions should be made with your licensed healthcare provider.

## The DSHEA disclaimer (verbatim, on ALL supplement content, even teasers)
> These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Consult your physician before starting any new supplement.

## FTC disclosures (verbatim, adjacent to the link, never in a footer)
- GLPulse (every mention): "#Disclosure — Dr. Hunter is a founder of GLPulse."
- myglp1cost.com / glp1checker.com: "#Disclosure — Dr. Hunter is the founder and creator of this tool."
- Third-party affiliate links: "#Affiliate — I may earn a commission if you purchase through this link."

## Testimonial rules
Written permission on file before use; no outcome guarantees; no medication-change stories; anonymized by default. Required line: "Shared with permission. Individual results may vary and are not guaranteed. Not intended as medical advice."

## Refund and cancellation policy (public)
- Platform monthly: cancel anytime from Settings, effective end of billing period.
- Platform annual: refundable within 14 days of purchase; pausable up to 3 months.
- Private engagements: private pay; payment plans available; audit fee credits toward tier enrollment within 60 days; rate lock for continuing clients renewing within 30 days.

## Privacy commitments (public, verifiable)
Age only (never DOB); no ads, trackers, or data sales; AI never sees identified health records; one-click account deletion; non-PHI platform layer with sensitive follow-up handled only in the covered Google Workspace clinical lane.

## Statistics rules
Every statistic carries its citation. Standing citations: two-thirds regain within a year of stopping = Wilding et al., 2021, NEJM. No compounded-semaglutide FDA-shortage framing (shortage resolved early 2025). Nothing uncited gets said, posted, or answered by any agent.

## Agent-specific guardrails (for any deployed brand agent)
1. Answer only from this catalog. If the catalog does not contain it, say so and offer the contact route.
2. Any question about symptoms, dosing, or a personal medical situation gets the redirect: educational resources + "that specific question belongs with your prescriber or healthcare provider."
3. Crisis or urgent-symptom language → immediately advise contacting their provider or emergency services. No exceptions, no continuation.
4. Never quote prices not in 02, never promise availability not in the calendar, never fabricate testimonials or statistics.
