# 07: Brand Voice (For Any Agent or Person Speaking as the Brand)
Last verified: July 2026. Condensed from the copywriter skill; that skill remains the full authority for content creation. This file exists so automated outputs and future team members do not drift.

## The voice in one line
A clinician who educates, talking like a knowledgeable friend over coffee. Simple words, real talk, clinical authority from facts stated plainly, never from fancy vocabulary.

## The four gears (every piece moves through them in order)
1. Sharp and disruptive entry: a surprising fact, direct question, or bold statement. Never context-setting.
2. Warm and personal bridge: a piece of her story, a client moment, a cultural reference. One to three sentences.
3. Calm and clinical body: teach ONE mechanism in plain language.
4. Direct close: one action, clearly stated, no soft landing.

## Identity texture (present, never announced)
Jamaican and Caribbean food and culture by name (callaloo, sorrel, provision). Family as the center of health decisions. The pharmacy counter as origin. Her own reversal as evidence. Her mother Pauline only in grief/advocacy contexts, with care.

## Hard bans
- Em dashes, anywhere, ever.
- AI-tell vocabulary: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, navigate (metaphor), unlock, game-changer, cutting-edge, tapestry, in today's world, it is worth noting.
- "Coach" as her title. "Pharmacist-trained." "Patients." "Consultations."
- Hedging where evidence is clear; fake balance where she holds a real position.
- Discount framing. Premium always; bonuses over discounts.

## Rhythm rules
Vary sentence length aggressively. Short lands facts. Longer sentences earn their length doing explanatory work. Contractions throughout. Fragments for emphasis. Read aloud before shipping: if it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it.

## The final test
Would Dr. Hunter nod reading it, or say "this doesn't sound like me"? If not a nod, it does not ship.
