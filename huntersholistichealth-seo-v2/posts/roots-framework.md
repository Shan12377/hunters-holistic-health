# The ROOTS Framework: How Hunter's Holistic Health Teaches Metabolic Health

Lasting health starts at the roots. That's not a tagline reaching for poetry; it's a description of how this education platform is structured. The ROOTS framework is the through-line connecting every tool, article, and lesson here, and this page explains what each letter actually means.

A quick, important note: Hunter's Holistic Health is a functional medicine **education** platform. ROOTS is a way of thinking and understanding, taught by Dr. Shallanda Hunter, CFNMP, PharmD, MBA. It is not clinical treatment, and it does not replace your medical team.

**Curious where to begin? Start with the R, [understanding your own numbers](/blog/understand-your-lab-numbers), using the free [tools on this site](/tools).**

## R — Review

*You cannot build on what you do not understand.*

Review is where everything starts. It means understanding your health history, your lab results in plain language, your medications, your supplements, and your daily habits as one connected picture rather than a stack of separate problems. Most people have carried their numbers for years without anyone translating them. Review closes that gap first, because every later decision depends on seeing clearly where you actually stand.

## O — Optimize Nutrition

*Not a generic handout. An education in how to eat for your body.*

The first O is nutrition education tailored to how an individual's metabolism works. It covers meal structure, the relationships between food and hormones, strategic meal timing, and culturally relevant food choices, so that what you learn fits your actual life rather than a template. The goal is understanding the "why" behind your plate, not following someone else's rigid rulebook.

## O — Optimize Biochemical Balance

*Supplement education. Interaction awareness. Quality standards.*

The second O is where a pharmacist's training earns its keep. This pillar is evidence-informed supplement education: understanding nutrient interactions, reading lab trends, and knowing quality standards like USP so you can tell a serious product from a marketing one. It is about informed decisions, made with your clinician, not a cabinet full of bottles bought on impulse.

*These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.*

## T — Transform Lifestyle Factors

*Most people never connect these dots. When you do, everything shifts.*

Transform covers the factors that quietly shape metabolic health: sleep quality, circadian rhythm, stress and cortisol awareness, movement, environmental toxin awareness, and the architecture of your daily habits. Individually these feel minor. Understood together, they explain a great deal, and that's the point of teaching them as a connected system rather than a list of tips.

## S — Sustain and Adapt

*Not a one-time fix. A way of thinking that stays with you.*

The final S is what separates a temporary push from a durable change. Sustain and Adapt means long-term monitoring of your own metabolic picture, course-correcting when life shifts, maintaining the habits that work, and building health infrastructure you keep for good. Life changes; a way of thinking adapts with it. That's the intended endpoint: not a program you finish, but a lens you keep.

## Why a Framework at All?

Health information online is endless and contradictory. A framework is simply a reliable order of operations, so you're not chasing the loudest trend but building understanding in a sequence that makes sense: understand yourself first (Review), then inputs (Nutrition, Biochemical Balance), then the surrounding system (Lifestyle), then durability (Sustain). Taught this way, the pieces reinforce each other instead of competing for your attention.

## Dr. Hunter's Perspective

I built ROOTS because, as a pharmacist, I kept meeting people drowning in health advice and starving for understanding. They didn't need another rule. They needed to comprehend their own bodies well enough to make confident, informed decisions alongside their clinicians. That's what this platform teaches, one root at a time. Everything else here, the tools, the articles, the lessons, is just ROOTS in practice.

## ROOTS Framework FAQs

### What does ROOTS stand for?

ROOTS stands for Review, Optimize Nutrition, Optimize Biochemical Balance, Transform Lifestyle Factors, and Sustain and Adapt. It is the educational framework taught at Hunter's Holistic Health by Dr. Shallanda Hunter, CFNMP, PharmD, MBA, structured as a logical sequence for understanding metabolic health.

### Is the ROOTS framework medical treatment?

No. Hunter's Holistic Health is a functional medicine education platform, and ROOTS is a way of thinking and understanding, not clinical care, diagnosis, or treatment. It's designed to help you understand your health and make informed decisions in partnership with your own medical team.

### Where do I start with ROOTS?

Start with R, Review, which means understanding your own numbers and health picture in plain language. The free tools and the article on reading your lab results are built for exactly this first step, because every later part of the framework builds on that foundation.

### Is ROOTS just about diet?

No. Nutrition is one of five parts. ROOTS also covers understanding your labs and history, evidence-informed supplement education, lifestyle factors like sleep and stress, and long-term sustainability. Treating it as a diet alone misses most of the framework.

### Who is the ROOTS framework for?

It's for people who want to genuinely understand their metabolic health rather than follow disconnected advice, and who want to make informed decisions alongside their clinicians. Because it's educational rather than clinical, it complements, and never replaces, professional medical care.
