## The definition

The ROOTS™ Framework is a five-phase functional and nutritional medicine education system that teaches people to understand and address the root patterns behind their metabolic numbers, created by Dr. Shallanda Hunter, PharmD, who built it while reversing her own metabolic condition.

The premise is simple: your numbers (blood pressure, fasting glucose, waist measurement, energy) are branches. Conventional care is very good at trimming branches in 10-minute appointments. Almost nobody teaches the root system underneath, because teaching takes time the system does not have. ROOTS is that teaching, structured.

## The five phases

**R: Review.** Start with your full picture: history, patterns, current numbers, medications, and life context. You cannot fix what you have not looked at, and most people have never had anyone look at all of it in one place.

**O: Optimize Nutrition.** Food as education, personalized to your actual kitchen and culture. Not a banned-foods list. Meal composition, sequencing, and timing, with the mechanism behind each explained.

**O: Optimize Biochemistry.** Supplements and how they interact with your medications, taught at PharmD depth. What things do, what the evidence grade is, and which combinations deserve caution, always as education to bring to your own providers.

**T: Transform Lifestyle.** Sleep, movement architecture, and stress chemistry: the three levers that move blood sugar and blood pressure directly, changed through evidence-informed structure rather than willpower theater.

**S: Sustain and Adapt.** Habits that hold for life, including what to do when progress stalls and how to adapt the system through seasons, travel, and setbacks. Lasting health starts at the roots, and lasting is the operative word.

## FAQ

**Is the ROOTS Framework medical treatment?**
No. It is structured education. Dr. Hunter is a PharmD acting as a functional medicine educator, not your prescribing physician. The framework is designed to make your conversations with your own providers better.

**Do I need lab work to start?**
No. The framework begins with numbers you already have access to: a blood pressure reading, a glucose meter reading, a tape measure, and your own patterns.

**Is ROOTS only for GLP-1 users?**
No. It is a metabolic health education system for anyone with unexplained numbers. The GLP-1 Exit Strategy is one application of it.

**Who created the ROOTS Framework?**
