## What is a GLP-1 exit strategy?

A GLP-1 exit strategy is the education and habit plan built before the last dose of a GLP-1 medication, so the patterns that raised your weight are not waiting at the finish line.

Most people on semaglutide or tirzepatide have a start date and no exit plan. The prescription came with instructions for taking it. Nobody handed over instructions for after.

## Why does an exit strategy matter? The numbers.

In the STEP 1 trial extension, participants who stopped semaglutide regained, on average, two-thirds of the weight they had lost within one year (Wilding et al., 2021, NEJM). Alongside that, body composition data from the semaglutide trials showed that a meaningful share of total weight lost was lean mass, not fat.

Put those together and the risk is specific: stop the medication with no plan, and you can end up at a similar weight to where you started, with less muscle than you started with. Less muscle means a smaller metabolic engine. That is the outcome an exit strategy exists to prevent.

None of this means the medications fail. They do their job. The gap is everything around and after them, and that gap is educational, which means it is fixable.

## The four parts of a GLP-1 exit strategy

### 1. Protein targets while you are losing
Muscle is rebuilt meal by meal in response to adequate protein arriving at once. During the appetite suppression of GLP-1 therapy, protein intake quietly collapses because everything gets eaten less. Protecting a per-meal protein floor (a palm-sized portion at each meal is the visual anchor) is the first defense of your lean mass.

### 2. Resistance work to keep the muscle
A calorie deficit takes fat and muscle unless the muscle gets a reason to stay. Resistance is the reason: three sessions a week, ten minutes each, bodyweight counts. Sit-to-stands, wall push-ups, carrying the heavy groceries deliberately. No gym required, and it matters most during the losing phase, not after it.

### 3. Blood sugar pattern education
The medication quiets appetite chemically. When it stops, appetite signaling returns to a body whose habits either changed or did not. Learning the levers now (meal sequencing, which cuts the post-meal glucose spike by roughly 30 to 40% in food-order studies; post-meal walking; fiber; sleep and stress, which move glucose directly) means the food noise returns to a person who has structure instead of only willpower.

### 4. The month-one-after plan, written before the last dose
The exit plan is a document, not a vibe: what your meals look like in week one after, what you track daily, what your movement floor is, and what number or feeling triggers a check-in with your prescriber. Written while you are still on the medication and thinking clearly, not improvised while appetite comes roaring back.

## What an exit strategy is NOT

It is not medication advice. Whether you stop, taper, or continue a GLP-1 long-term is a decision that belongs entirely to you and your prescriber, and current evidence supports many people staying on therapy long-term. An exit strategy is not an argument for stopping. It is the education that makes ANY path safer: if you continue, you keep more muscle and better patterns; if you and your prescriber decide to stop, you exit onto solid ground instead of thin air.

## When should you start building it?

The day you start the medication, and the second-best day is today. Every part of the strategy (protein, resistance, glucose education, the written plan) works while you are still on therapy, and each is harder to start after appetite returns.

## FAQ

**Will I definitely regain weight after stopping a GLP-1?**
No outcome is certain for an individual. The trial average is regaining about two-thirds of lost weight within a year (Wilding et al., 2021, NEJM), and averages include people who kept most of their loss. Habits built during therapy are the difference the research keeps pointing at.

**Does the weight come back as fat or muscle?**
Regain tends to be proportionally more fat than what was lost, which is why protecting lean mass during loss matters so much: it defends the engine you keep either way.

**Can I do an exit strategy without a coach or program?**
The components are free: protein floors, ten-minute resistance sessions, meal order, walking, a written plan. Structure and accountability are what programs add; the biology works regardless.

**Should I stop my GLP-1 to test my habits?**
No. Never change your medication without your prescriber. Bring your tracking data to that conversation and make the decision together.

**What does "food noise coming back" mean?**
GLP-1 therapy quiets intrusive food chatter chemically. After stopping, that signaling returns. People with structural habits (stable glucose, protein, sleep, cue plans) report managing it far better than people relying on willpower alone.
